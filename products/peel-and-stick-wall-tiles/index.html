FAQ

Common sourcing questions

Can wall tiles be used in bathrooms?

Many wall tiles are suitable for bathroom walls or vanity areas. Direct shower areas should be evaluated separately.

Do you support private label packaging?

Yes. We support retail boxes, inserts, barcode labels, and packaging claim review.

Can I request different surface styles?

Yes. Product options include 3D, stone-look, wood-look, vinyl, marble-look, and kitchen backsplash styles.

Can I get samples before bulk ordering?

Yes. Sample review is recommended for surface finish, adhesion, packaging, and installation guidance.
